SMS Messaging Consent

Last updated: April 1, 2026

This page explains how to opt in to SMS messaging from a Highmark-powered business, what you will receive, and how to stop at any time.

How to Opt In by Texting START

Businesses that use Highmark support keyword opt-in. You can begin receiving SMS messages from a Highmark-powered business by texting START to their number.

Opt-In Consent Language
By texting START to a Highmark-powered business number, you agree to receive SMS messages from [Business Name] via Highmark for customer support, booking assistance, and service updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

When you text START, the business will send a confirmation message before any other messages are sent. Here is an example of that exchange:

Messaging is optional. You are not required to opt in to receive SMS messages as a condition of making a purchase, booking a service, or accessing any business's offerings. Consent is entirely voluntary.

How Messaging Works

Highmark is an AI SMS concierge platform. We provide the messaging technology — businesses use it to communicate with their own customers.

1
You opt in

You opt in by texting START to a business's number, or by providing your phone number and agreeing to receive messages on a business's website or booking form.

2
You agree to receive messages

At the point of opt-in, clear consent language is displayed. You agree to receive SMS from that specific business only. Consent is not shared across businesses.

3
Highmark delivers the messages

Highmark's AI concierge sends and receives messages on behalf of the business. Messages relate only to your relationship with that business.

4
You stay in control

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. No further messages are sent. Reply START to re-enable if you change your mind.

Types of Messages You May Receive

Depending on your interaction, messages may include:

  • Customer support — answers to questions about services, pricing, and availability
  • Booking assistance — help selecting tours, rentals, appointments, or services
  • Confirmations — reservation or appointment confirmations after a booking
  • Service updates — relevant changes or updates related to your booking or inquiry
  • Follow-up — a single follow-up if your inquiry was not completed

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies based on your level of interaction with the business. You may receive a few messages during an active inquiry or booking process, and fewer or none after that. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

STOP, HELP, and START

Standard SMS keywords are always supported, regardless of the business or conversation state:

  • Reply STOP — immediately unsubscribes you from that business's messages. A single confirmation is sent; no further messages are sent after that.
  • Reply UNSTOP or START — re-enables messaging if you previously opted out.
  • Reply HELP — returns program information and a support contact number.

Opt-outs are processed before any other message handling. They are immediate and reliable.

Business Responsibility and Consent Scope

Each business that uses Highmark is individually responsible for:

  • Displaying clear opt-in disclosure language before messaging begins
  • Using messages only for the purposes disclosed at opt-in
  • Honoring opt-out requests immediately and permanently

Consent is never shared across businesses. If you consent to receive messages from Business A, that consent does not apply to Business B — even if both use Highmark. You only receive messages from businesses you have specifically engaged with and consented to.
