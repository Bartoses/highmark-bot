SMS Messaging Consent

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This page explains how Highmark's SMS messaging works, how consent is collected, and what you can expect when you receive a text from a Highmark-powered business.

How Messaging Works

Highmark is an AI SMS concierge platform. We provide the messaging technology — businesses use it to communicate with their own customers.

1
A business collects your phone number

You provide your number directly to the business — on their website, at point of booking, or by texting them first.

2
You agree to receive messages

At the point of collection, the business displays clear opt-in language. You consent to receive SMS from that specific business.

3
Highmark delivers the messages

Highmark's AI concierge sends and receives messages on behalf of the business. Messages relate only to your relationship with that business.

4
You stay in control

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. No further messages are sent. Reply START to re-enable if you change your mind.

Example Consent Language

Businesses using Highmark are required to display opt-in language similar to the following at the point where your phone number is collected:

"By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS messages from [Business Name] via Highmark for customer support, booking assistance, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Privacy Policy · Terms of Service"

Types of Messages

Depending on your interaction, messages may include:

  • Customer support — answers to questions about services, pricing, and availability
  • Booking assistance — help selecting tours, rentals, appointments, or services
  • Confirmations — reservation or appointment confirmations after a booking
  • Service updates — relevant changes or updates related to your booking or inquiry
  • Follow-up — a single follow-up if your inquiry wasn't completed

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your level of interaction with the business. You may receive a few messages during an active inquiry or booking process, and fewer or none after that. Message and data rates may apply.

How to Opt Out

You are always in control of whether you receive messages. Standard SMS keywords are supported:

  • Reply STOP — immediately unsubscribes you. A confirmation is sent; no further messages are sent.
  • Reply START or UNSTOP — re-enables messaging if you previously opted out.
  • Reply HELP — returns program info and a support contact.

These keywords work regardless of the business or conversation state. Opt-outs are processed before any other message handling.

Business Responsibility and Consent Scope

Each business that uses Highmark is individually responsible for:

  • Collecting your consent at the point of phone number collection
  • Displaying clear opt-in disclosure language before messaging begins
  • Using messages only for the purposes disclosed at opt-in

Consent is never shared across businesses. If you consent to receive messages from Business A, that consent does not apply to Business B — even if both use Highmark. You only receive messages from businesses you have specifically engaged with and consented to.
