Privacy Policy

About Highmark

Highmark is an AI SMS concierge platform operated by Whiteout Solutions ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide messaging technology that enables customer-facing businesses — including tour operators, rental companies, and service businesses — to communicate with their customers via SMS.

When you receive a text message from a business that uses Highmark, the message is sent on behalf of that specific business. Highmark processes the message as a platform provider; the business you engaged with is responsible for collecting your consent and ensuring messages are appropriate.

What Information We Collect

We collect information that is provided to us directly or generated through your use of the service:

  • Phone number — provided when you text or engage with a Highmark-powered business
  • Name — if you provide it during a conversation
  • Message content — the text of your conversations with the AI concierge
  • Booking and inquiry data — dates, group sizes, service preferences, and similar details you share
  • Interaction metadata — timestamps, message counts, session identifiers

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support on behalf of the business
  • Assist with booking, scheduling, and service information
  • Send confirmations, updates, and relevant service communications
  • Operate and improve the Highmark platform
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

How We Process Data on Behalf of Businesses

Highmark acts as a data processor on behalf of the businesses ("clients") that use our platform. Each client business is responsible for collecting your consent before you receive messages from them. Your data is used exclusively in the context of your relationship with that specific business.

Opt-in consent is business-specific. If you consent to receive messages from one Highmark-powered business, that consent does not carry over to any other business on the platform. Each business manages its own opt-in independently.

Your information is not sold. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data associated with one Highmark client is not shared with or visible to any other Highmark client.

Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With the business you contacted — the client business you engaged with has access to your conversation and contact info
  • With service providers — we use third-party services (Twilio for SMS delivery, Supabase for data storage, Anthropic for AI processing) that process data on our behalf under appropriate data agreements
  • As required by law — we may disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process

We do not share your data across unrelated businesses on the Highmark platform.

SMS Messaging, Consent, and Opt-Out

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from a Highmark-powered business, standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan. Message frequency varies based on your level of engagement with that business.

You can opt out of messaging at any time by replying STOP to any message. Your opt-out will be processed immediately. To re-enable messages, reply START or UNSTOP. For program information or support, reply HELP.

For full details on how opt-in and opt-out work, see our SMS Consent page.

Data Retention

We retain conversation data for as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Customers who opt out of messaging will not receive further messages, though some records may be retained for compliance purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt out of messaging at any time by replying STOP

To exercise these rights, contact us at sean@usehighmark.com.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy
