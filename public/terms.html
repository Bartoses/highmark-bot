Consent is not a condition of purchase. You are not required to opt in to receive SMS messages in order to make a purchase, complete a booking, or access any business's services. Messaging is entirely optional.

Types of Messages You May Receive

Depending on your interaction, you may receive messages related to:

  • Booking confirmations — confirmation of reservations you've made
  • Service updates — relevant changes or updates to your inquiry or booking
  • Follow-up — a single follow-up if your inquiry didn't result in a completed booking

Opting Out — STOP, START, and HELP

You can stop receiving messages at any time. Standard SMS keywords are always supported:

  • Reply STOP — immediately ends all messages from that business. One confirmation is sent; no further messages after that.
  • Reply START or UNSTOP — re-enables messaging if you previously opted out.
  • Reply HELP — returns program information and a support contact number.

Opt-outs are processed before any other message handling and take effect immediately.
